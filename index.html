FAQs

Questions businesses ask before choosing.

How do I know which machine my office needs? +
Start with the number of coffee drinkers and likely cups per day. The setup finder gives a practical guide, then Moyee can confirm the right machine, coffee supply and support package.
Can we lease instead of buying outright? +
Yes. The page supports outright purchase, lease or rental package quotes, and managed solutions where machine, coffee supply, support and service are discussed together.
Can Moyee supply the coffee too? +
Yes. Coffee supply can be added to any machine setup. The calculator helps estimate monthly kg so the quote can include the right amount of Moyee FairChain coffee.
Do you support events, activations, tastings and training? +
Yes. Moyee can support client events, corporate activations, ESG and sustainability initiatives, tree planting moments, tastings, product demos and coffee/barista training. These can be quoted separately or added to a managed coffee solution.
What if we only want to taste the coffee first? +
Use the tasting pack route. It is a lower-friction way to introduce Moyee coffee to the team before committing to a machine, lease package or managed supply setup.
What if our team size changes? +
Moyee can recommend a setup based on current usage and advise when it makes sense to upgrade machine tier, adjust coffee supply or add support.
